### 1) Business problem + why unlisted

MusicPro School (bundle it.musicproeventi.school, ASC 6806407450) is the digital companion for MusicPro Eventi / Associazione MusicPro, a single music school and cultural association in Italy. The app solves operational problems for our closed community: enrolled members and teachers view lessons and schedules, manage membership-related tasks, and (for authorized users) book rehearsal rooms. Access requires an account tied to our registry (associates, teachers, or approved room-booking customers)—there is no open signup for the general public.

Public App Store distribution is inappropriate because the product is not a consumer app for anyone browsing the Store; App Review confirmed Guideline 3.2. Unlisted distribution fits our model: we share one direct App Store link with new members and room customers (email, QR in our premises, onboarding from our website) while keeping the app out of search, charts, and category listings. Example: after enrollment, staff sends the unlisted link; the member installs the app and signs in with credentials we provision—without implying a public mass-market product.

### 2) Unlisted vs public

We do not want discovery by casual App Store users. The app only delivers value after our organization verifies membership or grants room-booking access. Listing publicly would attract downloads from people who cannot use the app, create support burden, and conflicts with Guideline 3.2. Unlisted gives a standard iOS install path on personal devices (BYOD) for our roster only, via a link we control, without presenting MusicPro School as a general-purpose app in Store browse or search.

### 3) Unlisted vs Apple Business / School Manager only

Our users are almost entirely on personally owned iPhones and iPads. We do not operate an MDM program or issue managed devices, and our members are not employees of a single enterprise enrolled in Apple Business Manager. Custom/private distribution through ABM/ASM would require organizational setup and managed-app workflows that our association and families cannot realistically use for BYOD enrollment.

Unlisted is the right balance: Apple-reviewed App Store install, direct link for onboarding, optional future use in ABM if we grow managed devices—but today we need a simple link for associates, teachers, and external room renters without forcing ABM as the only path.
