# Unlisted App Distribution — richiesta Apple (manuale)

Dopo il Submit for Review con note Unlisted (già fatto via API), Apple richiede anche il form:

https://developer.apple.com/contact/request/unlisted-app/

Account Holder / Admin → Safari sul Mac.

## Testo da incollare

App: MusicPro School  
Bundle ID: `it.musicproeventi.school`  
ASC App ID: `6806407450`  
Version: 1.1.0 (build 10)

MusicPro School is a member/teacher app for a single organization (MusicPro Eventi / Associazione MusicPro). Access is roster-based (associates and teachers in our school registry), not a general public consumer product. We previously received Guideline 3.2 feedback for public App Store distribution. We request Unlisted App Distribution so the app is available only via direct link (and Apple Business / Apple School Manager), not in App Store search, charts, or category listings. The current version is submitted to App Review with Review Notes stating the Unlisted intent.

## Dopo l’approvazione

In App Store Connect → Pricing and Availability: distribution method diventa **Unlisted App**. Condividi il link agli associati/docenti.
